Writing Chinese characters is hard. It's also beautiful and satisfying!

JuziGenius helps you master one skill: writing Chinese characters from memory. Reading Chinese characters is one thing, but recalling how to write it stroke-by-stroke is another. Most apps focus on reading, so we focus on handwriting.

Instead of boring flashcards, you'll write real sentences. Start with a few characters and unlock more as you go. Every new sentence only uses characters you already know, so you're always building on solid ground.

Use JuziGenius alongside your favorite study tools for grammar and reading — think of it as your cozy corner for putting pen to paper.

As for the name? It's a little pun. In Chinese, jùzi (句子) means "sentence" and júzi (橘子) means "orange." Isn't that genius? Juzi Genius!
